Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Short version: OpenAir TV has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your favourites stay on your own device.

Information we collect

None directly. You don't sign up or give us your name, email or any other personal details to use OpenAir TV.

Information stored on your device

Your favourite channels and the last channel you watched are saved in your browser or app on your own device, so they are there next time. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data or the app's storage.

Cookies and tracking

OpenAir TV does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Third-party services

To work, OpenAir TV connects to services run by others. Like any website, they can see basic technical details such as your IP address and device type:

  • Hosting: the website is hosted by Cloudflare, which may keep standard server logs for security and performance.
  • Channel list: loaded from iptv-org (hosted on GitHub).
  • Video streams and channel logos: load directly from each broadcaster's or provider's own servers.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Children

OpenAir TV does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Some channels may show content not suitable for all ages, so we recommend parental guidance.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email contact@openairtv.uk.
