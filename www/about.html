About OpenAir TV

Free live TV from around the world

OpenAir TV brings thousands of free, publicly available TV channels together in one simple place. Pick a category or search by name, and start watching. No account, no subscription, no fees.

What you can watch

News, sports, movies, music, kids' programmes, documentaries and much more, from broadcasters all over the world. Many broadcasters stream their channels for free online; OpenAir TV makes them easy to find.

Where the channels come from

The channel list comes from iptv-org, a community project that collects links to publicly available streams. OpenAir TV does not host, record or store any video. Each stream plays directly from its own source.

Because of this, some channels may be offline at times, run only part of the day, or be available only in certain countries.

Features

  • Search and browse by category
  • Save favourite channels on your device
  • Shareable links to any channel
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers, and can be installed as an app
